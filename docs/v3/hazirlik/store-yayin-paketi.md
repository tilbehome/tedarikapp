### 1.2 English

#### Name

`TedarikApp — 1688 Product Importer`

#### Short description — 115 characters

> Sends 1688 product data to your own TedarikApp panel on request, speeding up product research and list preparation.

#### Long description

TedarikApp — 1688 Product Importer is an internal tool that helps you send product information from a supported 1688 product detail page you are viewing to your own TedarikApp panel.

The extension previews supported page data such as the product title, source URL and product ID, price and MOQ information, variants, images, an available-video indicator, seller signals, package/carton details, and other supported product fields present on the page. Reading and sending begin only after your explicit action. Merely opening a supported product page does not automatically send the product to the panel.

This extension is not a standalone, general-purpose shopping or price-tracking application. You need access to a TedarikApp panel, the panel address, and an access token obtained from your own panel. Imported products are delivered to the TedarikApp inbox or list you select; further review, comparison, and list preparation take place in the panel.

The extension is not intended for a public consumer audience. It is distributed as unlisted in the Chrome Web Store and is intended only for authorized users who have the installation link.

### 2.2 English privacy policy

# TedarikApp Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE-DATE]`  
**Contact:** `[DESTEK-EPOSTA]`

#### Scope

This policy explains the data practices of the TedarikApp — 1688 Product Importer Chrome extension. The extension is designed to send product data from a supported 1688 product page being viewed by the user to the user's own TedarikApp panel, only through a user-initiated action.

#### What we do not collect

- The extension developer does not collect analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, or user-profiling data.
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment information, or similar personal data are not requested for the extension's purpose and are not intentionally processed.
- General browsing history is not monitored; content from unsupported pages is not collected or sent to the panel.
- Product data is not sold, shared, or transferred to advertising networks, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.
- The extension developer does not operate a separate collection or telemetry server for the extension.

#### What the extension does

When the user clicks the extension control on a supported 1688 product detail page, the extension reads supported product fields displayed on that page and shows a preview. Data is transmitted only after the user separately confirms sending, and only to the user's own TedarikApp panel configured in the extension settings. The extension does not initiate product transfers automatically.

Transferred data may include the product title, product/source identifier and URL, price and MOQ information, variants, declared stock, images and available video information, commercial seller signals displayed on the page, shipping details, and package/carton fields. The actual fields depend on what is present on the viewed page and supported by the extension schema.

#### Panel address and access token

The panel address is provided by the user. The access token is obtained from the user's own TedarikApp panel and is used only to establish an authorized connection to that panel. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or third-party services. Users should not share their token and should revoke and replace it in their panel if access is suspected.

#### Local storage and offline queue

The extension may store the panel address, access token, user settings, permission/consent state, and unsent products in an offline queue in the browser's extension storage. The queue exists so the transfer can be completed later through a user action. The deletion behavior for successfully sent queue entries and the maximum retention period must be aligned with the actual implementation before publication. `[DOĞRULA: queue deletion and retention period]`

Users may clear queue entries/settings through the extension settings or remove the extension to delete its browser extension data. `[DOĞRULA: names of the actual clearing controls in the UI]`

#### Transmission and panel retention

After the user confirms sending, product data is transmitted directly to the TedarikApp panel configured by the user. Production panel connections must be protected with HTTPS. `[DOĞRULA: HTTPS enforcement and any supported private/local panel exception]`

How long successfully imported product records remain in the panel, who can delete them, and how they are backed up are governed by the user's TedarikApp panel retention settings. The extension does not create a separate third-party archive of panel records.

#### No sale and purpose limitation

Data is used only for the extension's single purpose: transferring product research data. Data is not sold or used for credit eligibility, advertising targeting, or user profiling. Transfer to the user's own configured panel is the extension's core, clearly disclosed function.

#### Security

Extension permissions should be limited to the single purpose. The token and queued data should be accessible only in the extension context. Users should protect their browser profile on shared devices and revoke the token from the panel if unauthorized access is suspected.

#### Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date will be updated. Privacy or data-use questions may be sent to `[DESTEK-EPOSTA]`.

This extension reads product information from the 1688 product page you are viewing only after you click “Add to TedarikApp.” Data is sent to your own configured TedarikApp panel only after you separately approve the preview. No analytics or advertising tracking is used, and product data is not sent to another third party.
